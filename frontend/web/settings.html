Privacy
Local-first always on

Everything you say and every answer you give stays in this browser, on this device. Nothing syncs, there are no accounts, and there is no analytics of any kind.

Two things do leave the device, and you should know exactly what they are. Your voice: in Chrome and Edge, speech-to-text runs on the browser vendor's servers, not on your phone — your audio goes to them, not to us. And the wording of each check-in is written by an AI model we call, which sees the text of that one note. Neither can be switched off yet. If either bothers you, type instead of speaking, and know the app still works with the AI turned off.
